In-Game Laws

Members are not permitted to attack or kill another clan member on the Jedi Order Legacy team. Your lightsaber should be used to attack unauthorised or raiding hostiles.

Members are not permitted to use your lightsaber as a means of an in-game threat towards other clan members.

Exceptions:
- Temple Guard killing unauthorised clan members across the yellow, red or purple lines.
- Moderators killing people crowding a moderation scene after they have been warned to disperse.
- High Ranks killing someone for a valid reason.
Use polite and respectful language towards other members. Disrespect and offensive or insulting language is not allowed.

Treat all members with courtesy and respect, regardless of personal opinions or rank.

Members should always be held accountable for their actions. We want to create a feeling of responsibility and safety within the community.

Members using the Jedi Order Legacy tag in-game must not t-bag / ro-bag any other user.

Members must always follow the Roblox TOS while in-game.
The use of cheats, hacks, or exploits is strictly prohibited.

You must not take advantage of bugs or glitches in-game. The use of an unintended in-game glitch to give yourself an unfair advantage over other users is prohibited.

Use of third-party software is prohibited while in-game.

Use of an alt account to evade moderation actions taken on one of your accounts it prohibited.
Clan members are not allowed to use a red lightsaber when using the Jedi Order Legacy clan tag.

Members must not wear any attire that resembles the Sith in any way.

Members should note, Sith are not allowed within the temple and The Sith Eclipse is Kill on Sight.

Using your lightsaber to attack innocent or peaceful hostiles in the temple is not allowed.

Hostiles may not interrupt or kill any attendees in an event, training or trial.
- Hostiles should note, punishment will vary on; how many people were killed, if the user had clear intentions and the size of the event.
Members are not permitted to cross the yellow, red or purple lines in front of the council chambers without permission.

Members are not permitted to enter the holding cells unless their rank or authorisation permits it.

Members are not permitted to sit on the council chairs located in the council chambers. Reserved for Jedi Council members.

You can find all permitted ranks in the Temple Access card.
Members are urged to report any violations they see within the clan.
Anything that breaks Clan or NWG rules should be reported with proof through the proper means.
Members are also urged to let moderators know of any violations.
Abuse of any sort of admin powers while in game is not allowed. Moderators are not to use their powers in an abusive or unfair way.
Abuse of Btools while in-game is strictly prohibited.
Moderators should always follow the punishment index and moderation handbook.
Bans should be logged in the appropriate place.
Admin commands are not to be used by moderators unless absolutely necessary.
Moderators dealing with tickets should always ask if the user who opened the ticket is finished.
Any member with in-game admin powers is not to use a command on a user without proper reason, abuse, or gain unfair leverage.

Discord Rules

Treat all members with courtesy and respect, regardless of personal opinions or rank.

Disrespect is not permitted within the clan.
Members are not to engage in hate speech or any form of discrimination based on race, religion preference, or sexuality.
Do not engage in actions that could be considered harassment, including threats, doxxing, or any behaviour intended to intimidate or upset others.
Sending any explicit or inappropriate content (NSFW) within any of Jedi Order Legacy owned servers is strictly prohibited.

If you think what you're about to send may be NSFW; chances are it is and don't send it.
Members should not send excessive messages, flooding channels with irrelevant content, or using excessive pings.

Do not promote other servers, products, or services without direct and clear permission from the Grandmaster and above.

No Illegal Content: Do not share or distribute illegal content, including illegal drugs, counterfeit items, or stolen financial information.

No Threats: Do not make threats of violence or harm towards other members.

No Doxing: Do not share or threaten to share someone's personal information at all.
When using a channel, members must:
- Follow Channel Guidelines: Adhere to the specific rules for each channel, including topic restrictions and permitted content.
- Be mindful of background noise and avoid excessive talking or disruptions in voice channels.
- Never play NSFW sounds or music in voice channels.
- Never impersonate other users, especially High Ranking members.
- Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines: Adhere to Discord's official rules and guidelines.
Any member with moderation powers is not to use a command on a user without proper reason, abuse their admin, or try to gain unfair leverage.

Moderators should always follow the punishment index and moderation handbook when acting on a user who has breached clan rules.

Moderators are not to use warn, mute, kick, or ban commands without valid reason.

Deleting moderation logs or ticket transcripts in secret or without authorisation is strictly prohibited.

Punishment Index

Minor Infractions
Examples: Spam, mild disrespect, minor trolling, inappropriate usernames. (Additionally anything else not listed here that is in the Jedi Order Legacy Offense Sheet Doc within the “Minor Infractions” area.)

Actions:
First Offense: Verbal warning.
Second Offense: Timeout (Discord: mute 10-30 minutes, Game: verbal warning - if continued a game kick).
Third Offense: Temporary mute (1 hour+), or temp game ban (12-24 hours).
Document repeat offenders in both the Moderation server and the Jedi Order Legacy server for pattern tracking.

Major Infractions
Examples: Mass Team Kill, Threatening, Map Abuse, continuous Interruption of training, Ban Evasion, Hacking, Admin Abuse, Doxxing, Slander in any form, Severe Toxicity, Impersonation of any High Ranks within JOL in order to gain access to restricted areas, NSFW, Freeranking, Raiding Jedi Order Legacy Temple while attaining a High rank within Jedi Order Legacy, Blackmailing. All punishments for these (apart from Doxxing in any capacity, hacking, discrimination in any capacity, severe toxicity, slandering in any form - these are all permanent bans) are individual and it is mandatory you educate yourself on each rule before administering punishments onto any user.

Actions:
First Offense: Immediate ban corresponding to the correct punishment within Jedi Order Legacy Offense Sheet Doc. (And then document the incident using the format within "Documentation and Reporting" accurately).
Second Offense of any temporary bans after they have been banned for a temporary major offense violation already - 20 day ban.
Third Offense - Permanent ban. (Log the situation with the "Documentation and Reporting" format below accurately).
If doxxing or real-life threats occur, report to Discord Trust & Safety and permanently after permanently banning the perpetrator. (Log situation with the format below accurately)
Level 1 — Minor Offences
Examples include spam, minor trolling, off-topic behavior, or mild disrespect.
First Offense: Verbal warning.
Second Offense: Temporary Mute (10 Minutes) / Kick from the game.
Third Offense: 1 day ban (Coming back after a kick and continuing to break the rules).

Level 2 — Major Offences (Non-Permanent)
Examples include harassment, filter bypassing, targeted toxicity, impersonation (non-malicious), repeated minor offenses.
First Offense: Punishment corresponding to their offense.
Second Offense: 20 day ban.
Third Offense: Permanent ban.

Level 3 — Critical Offences
(This excludes hacking, NSFW, Doxxing, discrimination in any regard)
First Offense: 3-7 Day Ban.
Second Offense: Permanent Ban Review.

What is the Jedi High Council?
The Jedi High Council is a group of six members who make critical choices for the clan. They sit at the top of the clan, serving the community. Each Jedi High Council member has a position in overseeing a pathway or clan ranks.

How do I become a Jedi High Council member?
To become a Jedi High Council member is a long and rewarding process. First, you must complete an application sent out when a spot on the council is open. If you then pass said application, you will be placed on a clan and high rank vote. The top people in those two votes will then be voted on by the community in a final voting process.

The Shadow Pathway are an elite group of warriors within the clan. They are responsible for guarding the main gates, hosting team trainings and taking part in special raids. The Shadow pathway is always combat ready and expected to have enhanced combat abilities.

Raiding and Defence Guide

Clause 1 - Map Abuse

Map abuse is prohibited for the raiding and defending party. Areas that are considered map abuse in the Jedi Order Legacy Temple during raids:
- Water surrounding spawn.
- The hole above spawn.
- Any out-of-bounds areas in the map.
*Users with access to building tools (Btools) may use them to block these areas during or before raids.

Clause 2 - Spawn Camping:

The defending and raiding party are not allowed to spawn camp. This means that a single person and/or group of people using the enemy spawn to continue to kill them - giving an unfair advantage to one side. If a specific clan has raiding and spawn camped before, and has repeated doing so, they themselves may be spawn camped when raided by Jedi Order Legacy.

Clause 3 - Mutual respect:

When raiding or defending, people with the Jedi Order Legacy clan are expected to fight with honour and not making toxic or unlawful comments against the other party. Jedi Order Legacy are to raid and defend in accordance to the Law of Civility under In-game Laws. Making the sparce comment against the other party during a raid is allowed, it is the over or extreme use of such negative comments that we do not tolerate.

Clause 4 - Use of Admin
During a raid, admin is not allowed unless it is for a lawful reason.
For example:
- Moderating an Exploiter
- Using building tools (Btools) to block areas that count as map abuse.
- Moderating a user who is breaking our raiding laws.
- Using commands on yourself if spectating and the target if moderating.
